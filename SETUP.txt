This package includes the three files that must stay in sync:

Workflow behavior:
- Runs automatically on every push/commit to main in THIS profile repository.

- Publishes SVGs with a brand-new filename on every run, bypassing GitHub's image cache.

Recommended commit message:

IMPORTANT:
Replace all three files above together. Do not keep an older render_profile.py.
